# StudyHub Demo Data Report

## Purpose
This dataset is populated for a frontend demo. It is not an official or batch-verified NIT Raipur syllabus. Subjects and unit outlines are representative engineering-course examples.

## Included
- 9 departments × 8 semesters = 72 JSON files.
- 234 representative subjects and 936 illustrative units.
- Each unit includes a syllabus-style outline and two working search links: Google notes/PDF search and YouTube lecture/playlist search.
- Buttons are labeled **Search notes** and **Find videos** because these URLs open search results, not a hand-verified specific PDF or video.

## Important limitations
- Course names and unit outlines are demo content, not confirmed NIT Raipur curriculum.
- Search URLs are generated topic searches; individual results have not been verified or manually selected.
- Do not present the dataset as an official syllabus. Before production, replace demo data with your intended curriculum and direct resources.

## Data location
`react/data/json/<department>/semester-<n>.json`
